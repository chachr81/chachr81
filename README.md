### 🛰️ About Me
- Remote Sensing specialist and Professor of Geography with more than **10 years of experience** in **spatial database modeling, design, and management** using **PostgreSQL/PostGIS, Transact-SQL, PL/SQL**, and **ESRI Enterprise Geodatabase**.  
- Developer of **back-end architectures for Web GIS applications**, integrating **FastAPI**, REST services, geospatial APIs, ETL workflows, and automated pipelines using **Python** and **R**.  
- Extensive experience in **LiDAR processing**, environmental analysis, spatial modeling, and high-resolution geospatial workflows on both Linux and Windows.  
- Focused on modern GIS development: distributed computation, cloud-based geospatial analysis, and scalable spatial infrastructures.
